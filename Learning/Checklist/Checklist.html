T1 v1.5 · 2026-08-23 ● ACTIVE collapse / expand

The checklist — 9 gates, run before every trade

  1. ASTRO — day graded on FORECAST (Watch / Prepare / Execute). Astro sizes expectation and volatility — never direction.
  2. ICC + HTF VETO — bias from 4H → 1H → 15m. Indication = body close past the previous push; wicks don't count. Enter on continuation, never the indication candle. Check the 4H map first: never trade INTO your own A++ zone, HTF pocket, or unswept draw.
  3. VOLUME — name the condition: balanced or imbalanced, on which timeframe (profile shape, VWAP slope, inside or outside value). Levels from prior established value only: composite VA edges, naked POCs, HVN edges, DVA/AVWAP bands — weighted by profile size. Acceptance = time + space; a wick is a rejection. Never fade the 2nd SD in a trending session. Order flow is confirmation at a level, never the reason.
  4. STDV — manipulation leg off a key open (midnight EST · 4H · 18:00 futures · Sunday weekly), marked 5m wick high → wick low. Session highs & lows marked (Tokyo · London · NY · prior day). Rungs: −2/−2.5 decision · −3.5/−4 completion · −4/−4.5 extended. Rungs are targets to trade INTO — at or past −1 the entry is late. An unmitigated FVG between entry and the rung is a scheduled dip: enter AT the gap, never above it. Overnight: wait for the midnight leg. Never chase past −4.
  5. OTE — 3m marking: tick high → body of the lowest wick. Pocket .705–.786; the .705 is the trigger line. Rolling anchor on the tap-and-dip. Fallback .5/.618/.66 only with a marked rejection level. An OTE is dead once price breaks its 1.
  6. MD — the 13 gates pass. The discretion layer (FVG/iFVG · CE · order block · breaker · rejection block) confirms inside the zone — displacement or LTF MSS, never the bare touch. No SMT, no expansion. TP depth from the liquidity map, not the fib.
  7. TRIGGER — a liquidity pool was swept behind the entry. Fade the swept side. Entry form: the 1m FVG snapback.
  8. RISK — stop beyond the sweep zone / structural invalidation, never mid-gap. The stop moves only toward price — never widened, never cancelled. Never park a stop inside or just past an unmitigated FVG. If a scheduled dip turns against the position, close it. Partial at TP1 always, breakeven after. Split A+B entries share one trade's total risk.
  9. DISCIPLINE — no chasing: re-entry only at the original zone or better. After a loss: size down or stand down, never up. One emotional action = session over. 2–3 failed trades in one day = lock the account until tomorrow. No FOMO, no overleveraging, no taking off early.
