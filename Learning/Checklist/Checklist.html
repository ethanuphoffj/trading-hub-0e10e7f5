The simplified checklist — 8 gates, run before every trade

  1. ASTRO — day graded on FORECAST (Watch / Prepare / Execute). Astro sizes expectation and volatility — never direction.
  2. ICC + HTF VETO — bias from the 4H → 1H → 15m cascade; indication = body close past the previous push (wicks don't count); enter on continuation, never the indication candle. Check the 4H map FIRST: never trade INTO your own A++ zone, HTF pocket, or unswept draw — an LTF setup pointing into the HTF magnet makes you the liquidity.
  3. STDV — manipulation leg off a key open (midnight EST · 4H · 18:00 futures · Sunday weekly), marked 5m wick high → wick low. Mark out session highs & lows (Tokyo · London · NY · prior day) — the pools price hunts. Rungs: −2/−2.5 = decision zone · −3.5/−4 = completion · −4/−4.5 extended. Rungs are targets to trade INTO, never entries in the move's direction — at/past −1 you're late. Mind the retrace path: an unmitigated FVG between entry and the rung is a scheduled dip — enter AT the gap, never above it (it must survive your stop + eval headroom). Overnight: wait for the midnight leg to print and key it. Never chase past −4.
  4. OTE — 3m marking: tick high → body of the lowest wick. Pocket .705–.786, the .705 is the trigger line. Rolling anchor: the tap-and-dip becomes the next fib's 1. Fallback ladder .5/.618/.66 only with a marked rejection level. An OTE is dead once price breaks its 1.
  5. MD — the 13 gates pass. A mechanical level is never enough alone: the discretion layer (FVG/iFVG · CE · order block · breaker · rejection block) must confirm inside the zone — displacement candle or LTF MSS, never the bare touch. No SMT, no expansion. TP depth comes from the liquidity map, not the fib.
  6. TRIGGER — a liquidity pool was swept behind the entry (the one backtest-proven ICT rule). Direction = fade the swept side. Entry form = the 1m FVG snapback inside the pocket.
  7. RISK — stop beyond the sweep zone / structural invalidation, never mid-gap. The stop is sacred: it moves only toward price (BE / trail) — never widened, never cancelled. Stops respect the gap map: never park a stop inside or just past an unmitigated FVG — quick moves leave gaps, price comes back for them (double after breakouts). If a scheduled dip turns against the position: close it — the quick trade TO the gap beats holding through it. Partial at TP1 always, breakeven after. Split A+B entries share one trade's total risk.
  8. DISCIPLINE — no chasing: re-entry only at the original zone or better. After a loss: size down or stand down — never up. One emotional action = done for the session; write the log instead. 2–3 failed trades in one day = LOCK THE ACCOUNT — done until tomorrow, no fourth attempt. Let the market do its thing — no FOMO, no overleveraging, no taking off early. (This gate blew the Aug 9 eval: −$2,200 — while the drawn short would have PASSED it.)
