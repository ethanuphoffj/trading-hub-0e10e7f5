CANDLESTICKS — the language of the market

Every candle is a sentence about who fought and who won that session. Patterns repeat because human behavior under fear, greed, and hope is patterned. The whole 168-page system reduces to one skeleton — the same one ICC runs on: find the trend, find the level, wait for the signal AT the level, then let money management carry the edge.

ANATOMY

Reading one candle

A candle = open · high · low · close. Color is irrelevant — the relative position of open and close is everything. Long body = strong pressure. Small body = little conviction. Long shadow = a battle fought far from the close, and lost.

THE THREE EVENTS

Every pattern is one of three things

REJECTION — one side pushed to an extreme and was thrown back → trade against the rejected side
CAPITULATION — one side fully consumed the other → trade with the consuming side
INDECISION — nobody won, the market is coiling → the NEXT candle decides, not this one
REJECTION: PIN BARS · DOJIS · TWEEZERS — CAPITULATION: ENGULFING · STARS — INDECISION: DOJI · INSIDE BAR
THE PATTERNS

The ten that matter

Psychology first, names second — if you know why the candle formed, you know what happens next. Every read below assumes the pattern printed AT a key level; away from a level, ignore them all.

Hammer (bullish pin bar)

REJECTION · BULLISH

Sellers pushed after the open, buyers overwhelmed them, close back at the top. At support / MA / fib after a decline = reversal long.

Shooting star (bearish pin bar)

REJECTION · BEARISH

Buyers pushed, sellers erased it. At resistance in an uptrend = reversal short — one of the book's highest-probability signals.

Bullish engulfing

CAPITULATION · BULLISH

Selling power fully consumed. At the end of a downtrend = capitulation bottom; mid-uptrend = continuation. Nison: needs a definable trend + full engulfment.

Bearish engulfing

CAPITULATION · BEARISH

Buyers engulfed by sellers at the highs — the uptrend's obituary when it prints at resistance.

Doji

INDECISION

Perfect equality — nobody in control. After a strong leg = the trend is losing its engine: take profits. An entry only with confluence.

Dragonfly & Gravestone doji

REJECTION AT THE EXTREME

Open=close AND at the extreme: an entire session of one-sided pressure, absorbed. Dragonfly at support = long; gravestone at resistance = short. Location is mandatory.

Morning star

CAPITULATION · 3 CANDLES

Bear body → small pause → bull body closing above the first body's midpoint. Control transferred. Bottom-of-downtrend at support = powerful reversal long.

Evening star

CAPITULATION · 3 CANDLES

The mirror: bull → pause → strong bear. Top-of-uptrend at resistance = reversal short. The pause candle can be anything — doji, spinner, either color.

Harami / inside bar

INDECISION · DUAL NATURE

The market coils. Strong trend: continuation — trade the mother-bar break, stop beyond its far side. At a top/bottom: reversal. After a level break: "breakout not confirmed yet."

Tweezers top / bottom

REJECTION · MATCHED EXTREMES

Second session opens where the first closed and goes straight the other way — the level held twice. Bottom at support = long; top at resistance = short.

Inside bar FALSE breakout

THE STOP-HUNT SIGNATURE

Institutions push through the obvious level, harvest the clustered stops, then drive the REAL move. Entry on the close back inside the mother range; stop beyond the fake-out extreme. The book's best R:R setup — risk 50 to make 400.

The validity checklist

BEFORE TRADING ANY PATTERN

1 — Formed on 4H/daily (1H minimum)?
2 — In line with the dominant trend?
3 — AT a key level (S/R · S&D · 8/21 MA · 50–61.8% fib · trendline)?
4 — Clean anatomy (tail ≥ 2× body · full engulfment · baby fully inside)?
5 — ≥ 2:1 R:R to the next opposing level?

Any "no" → skip the trade, however beautiful the candle.

MARKET STRUCTURE

Three regimes, three playbooks

Diagnose on the 4H/daily/weekly — never on small frames. Trends run ~30% of the time; ranges ~70%; chop is unclassifiable noise you don't trade at all.

Trending: two moves only — impulsive (with trend) and corrective. The previous swing point or a clean 2-touch trendline (4H/daily, never forced) marks where the next impulse is born.
Ranging: confirm 2 touches per boundary, then three plays — fade the boundaries · trade the breakout · wait for the pullback to the broken level. Boundary overshoots trap breakout traders constantly.
Choppy: no definable boundaries, tight noise — zoom out on the daily to diagnose, then close the chart. Chop is where winners give back their profits.
Top-down veto: weekly → daily → 4H. A perfect signal on your timeframe DIES at a higher-timeframe level — the book's flagship lesson: a textbook daily pin bar bought straight into weekly resistance, and lost.
THE FOUR STRATEGIES

One frame, four triggers

1

Pin bar — the rejection trade

Trend + pullback + pin AT the level (S/R, or the 21 MA as dynamic S/R when no static level exists). Two entries: aggressive — at the pin's close, never miss the move · conservative — limit at 50% of the pin's range, 5:1-class R:R when it fills. Stop beyond the tail; target the next opposing level. The rejection isn't the signal — the location is.

2

Engulfing bar — the capitulation trade

Same frame, stacked confluence: S/R flips · 8/21 MA pullbacks · 50–61.8% fib ("most major moves retrace 50–61%") · trendlines · supply & demand zones (stronger than S/R — grade them by speed of departure, R:R offered, and 4H/daily origin). In ranges: boundaries, breakout + pullback, or the false-break fade.

3

Inside bar — the coil trade

Strong trend pauses → trade the break of the mother bar with the trend; stop beyond the mother's far side. After an S/R breakout, an inside bar means the breakout is not confirmed yet — enter on the inside bar's break, not the level's. Big timeframes, dominant trend, key levels, confluence — or skip.

4

Inside bar false breakout — the stop-hunt trade

Banks know where the stops cluster: beyond the obvious level. They push through, harvest the liquidity, and drive the real move the other way. When the break closes back inside the mother range, the trap is sprung — enter WITH the institutions, stop beyond the fake-out extreme. The bull-trap at range resistance is the classic. This is the book's crown jewel and its best R:R.

MONEY MANAGEMENT

"The key to the castle"

minimum 2:1 reward:risk — or the trade doesn't exist
risk ≤ 1–2% of equity per trade · think in dollars, not pips
hard stops always — mental stops are account death
STOP GOES BEYOND THE PATTERN'S INVALIDATION — PIN TAIL · MOTHER BAR · FAKE-OUT EXTREME — NEVER AT AN ARBITRARY DISTANCE
🧮The math IS the edge. Ten trades at 3:1, risking $200 each. Lose seven, win three: −$1,400 + $1,800 = +$400 while losing 70% of the time. You don't need to be right — you need to be paid more when you are. Set the stop and target at entry, then don't look back: the market tells you if you're right, your emotions don't.
