🔢 NUMEROLOGY

The numbers and how to compute them

Every date carries two numbers, and both are read before and after reduction — the raw compound number has its own meaning, and so does the reduced digit.

How to calculate

Life path of the day (full-date sum): add every digit of month + day + full year. Example: Aug 5 2026 → 8+5+2+0+2+6 = 23 → 2+3 = 5. The raw total (23) is the compound; the reduced value (5) is the life path.

Day number: the calendar day kept whole first (the 20th is a 20 before it is a 2), then reduced. The compound day number carries its own meaning.

Masters are never reduced: 11, 22, 33 stay as they are. Hidden masters: a raw total or day of 20 or 29 contains a hidden 11 (2+0, 2+9→11); 22 is a double 11. Don't collapse masters prematurely — a component sum like 11+11+22 = 44 → 8 is "an 8 carrying triple master energy."

🀄 CHINESE ASTROLOGY

Pillars, elements, trines, clashes

Chinese astrology runs on a 60-unit cycle: 10 heavenly stems (5 elements × yin/yang) paired with 12 earthly branches (the animals). Years, months, days, and hours each carry a pillar. The day pillar is the core identity — for a person or for a date.

The five elements and their two cycles

CycleSequenceWhat it describes
Generation (sheng)Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → WoodEach element feeds/produces the next. "X generates Y" = X is Y's resource.
Control (ke)Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → WoodEach element dominates one other. "X controls Y" = X restrains/exploits Y. In BaZi wealth theory, the element you control is your wealth element.

The four trines (harmony groups)

TrineAnimalsArchetype
WaterRat · Dragon · MonkeyVisionary/leader — status, authority, adaptive influence.
MetalSnake · Rooster · OxBuilder — classically the wealth trine: assets, compounding, patience.
FireDog · Tiger · HorseWarrior — passion-manifestation, aggression, loyalty.
WoodRabbit · Pig · GoatDiplomat/creative — information, networks, gentleness.

The six clashes (direct oppositions)

Rat ↔ Horse · Ox ↔ Goat · Tiger ↔ Monkey · Rabbit ↔ Rooster · Dragon ↔ Dog · Snake ↔ Pig

A clash between two pillars (a day's animal against a chart's animal, or the year's) is read as friction/disruption between them — traditionally the most destabilizing single relation. What that disruption *does* to a market is your pattern to find.

How to read a day against a chart

1 — Elements: take the day pillar's element and the chart's element; name the relation from the two cycles (same / generates / is generated by / controls / is controlled by). That's the energetic wiring for the day.
2 — Animals: check the day's branch against the chart's animal — same animal, same trine, clash, or nothing. Trine = harmony group; clash = the friction pair.
3 — The year sits over everything: 2026 is the Fire Horse — run the same two checks between the year and each chart once, and it colors the whole year.
4 — Wealth element note: the element a chart CONTROLS is its classical wealth element — days/years of that element are traditionally its "wealth activation" (e.g. Water controls Fire → a Water chart in a Fire year).

🌙 MOON & ECLIPSES

One cycle ≈ 29.5 days, new moon to new moon. The sheet always shows the phase, illumination %, and waxing/waning — here's what the words mean.

PhaseWhat it isTraditional reading
🌑 New moonMoon between Earth and Sun — dark skyBeginnings, planting, initiations. Shem's market claim: rises tend to begin here.

🌕 Full moonEarth between Sun and Moon — fully litCulmination, harvest, emotional peak. Traditional claim: reversals and drops cluster near it. Subconscious wide open.
🌖–🌘 WaningIllumination shrinking toward newRelease, completion, defense, clearing.

Eclipses — what they actually are

Solar eclipse = a new moon exact enough that the Moon covers the Sun. Lunar eclipse = a full moon exact enough that Earth's shadow covers the Moon. Astrologically they're read as supercharged versions of their phase — sudden, fated turning windows, traditionally volatile and "not normal days." They come in pairs (a solar and lunar within ~2 weeks). Whether an eclipse means reversal, acceleration, or nothing for a given asset — that's exactly the kind of pattern your journal exists to catch.

Mercury retrograde — what it IS

Three or four times a year, Mercury appears to move backward against the stars for ~3 weeks (an optical effect of orbital speeds — nothing physically reverses). Traditional reading: communication, technology, contracts, and travel get glitchy — misreads, fakeouts, do-overs. The trading-lore version: fakeouts and fat-fingers increase. It's the most famous — and most disputed — cycle in popular astrology, which makes it a perfect candidate for your own logs.
