📖 The Philosophy

Information, not prediction. The hub lays out each day's facts — numbers, pillar, moon, dignities — and never says long or short. You observe, you log, you draw the patterns. Market-only: the asset's chart vs the date, never the trader's.

🕐 The Segments

  • NUMBERS — life path + day number, raw compounds, masters/hidden 11s, the reference codes (7 · 11 · 28 · 22).
  • CHINESE — the day pillar vs each asset: element cycles, trines, clashes.
  • MOON — phase, verified windows, eclipses (what they are, not what they demand).
  • WESTERN — season, ruler dignities, Mercury motion, planetary day.

📓 The Pattern Journal

The almanac is the dictionary; the journal is the doctrine. Log each day's sheet against what each asset actually did — contradictions ("a 28 that shorted") are the product, not noise. Per asset, sample size before conviction.
